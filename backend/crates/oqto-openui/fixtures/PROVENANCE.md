# Conformance fixtures

The six directories from `compare-options` to `plan-approve` and `invalid-unknown` are the
Oqto OpenUI profile's shared conformance fixtures, copied unchanged from
`@byteowlz/oqto-openui` 0.2.2 (`fixtures/`, commit 1253054; the tarball oqto vendors in
`frontend/vendor/oqto-openui/`, sha256 7452eb44...). License: `LICENSE-oqto-openui`.

`core-rollout` and `core-ui-part` are Oqto's own: the byoagnt chat example's rollout part
and the session-view `ui-part` scenario's part, both in the core set.
